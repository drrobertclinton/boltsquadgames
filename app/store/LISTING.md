## Description
Deep under the sea, your squad of robots keeps the base running. But one of them is the Glitch.

Fix the base. Feed the fish in the Coral Lab, calibrate the sonar, fill the sub ballast and count the pearls in the vault.

Find the Glitch. Scan robots, watch the security cameras, and call an emergency meeting when something seems off. Then everyone votes.

Or BE the Glitch. Sneak through the vents, cause trouble, and try not to get caught.

• Play with friends: start a squad and share the code. Friends on the app or on boltsquadgames.com can join.
• Practice vs robots: smart robot players on Easy, Normal or Hard, anytime, even offline.
• Special jobs: Engineer and Tracker each have their own powers.
• Dress up your robot: 71 hats, 17 outfits, paints, visors, and a face picture of your own.
• Leaderboards: daily, weekly, monthly and all-time.
• 2-minute tutorial teaches everything.

Made for kids and families:
• No ads and nothing to buy.
• Chat uses ready-made phrases only, so no one can type messages to your child.
• Your robot's face picture stays on your device. It is never uploaded or shown to other players.

## Review notes (App Store "Notes", Google "App access")
No sign-in is needed. Tap Practice vs Robots to play right away, or Tutorial to learn in 2 minutes. Play with Friends needs a second device: tap Start a Squad on one and Join with the code on the other. Face pictures are optional and stay on the device in this app; they are never uploaded or shared. (Web players' pictures are approved by our team before anyone sees them, and any player can report one from the meeting screen.) Chat is preset phrases only.

### Kids and privacy
A photo of a child is personal information under COPPA. The app avoids collecting it by asking for pets, toys and drawings and by denying any picture of a person. The parent's store purchase behind the grown-up gate, plus the consent box, is the parent's OK for the upload. Deletion requests: robert@vilasi.co. This is a practical setup, not legal advice.
