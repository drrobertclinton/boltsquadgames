Bolt Squad Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version. Bolt Squad has no ads and no tracking. The only thing to buy is an optional photo pass in the apps, which a grown-up buys through Apple or Google. We never sell information. We only keep what the game needs to work, like your robot's name and your saved progress. Photos are never shown to other players until a grown-up from our team approves them.

Bolt Squad is made by Vilasi, Inc. ("we"). This policy covers the Bolt Squad game on the web at boltsquadgames.com and in the Bolt Squad apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

What the game stores on your device

Your robot, hats, coins, settings and progress are saved on your own device so you can keep playing.

What we keep on our servers

  • Cloud save. Your progress is backed up under a random save code so you can move it to another device. It holds your robot's name and game progress, not your real name, email or phone number.
  • Leaderboard. If you finish games, your robot's name and score can appear on the Bolt Squad leaderboard. Names are filtered for bad words.
  • Robot face pictures (optional). You can choose a picture for your robot's face. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it. A shared picture goes to our team first, and other players only see it after a grown-up approves it. Any player can report a picture, and a reported picture is hidden right away until we check it.
  • Photo passes (apps, version 1.1 and later). In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, sharing a face picture with other players needs a photo pass. Only a grown-up can buy one: the app first asks a grown-up question and for a parent or guardian to agree, then Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see card details. We keep the store's transaction number with the shared picture so we know it was paid. Please share pets, toys, drawings or things, not people's faces; we deny pictures of people. If we deny a picture, the photo pass comes back.
  • Idea Box (optional). If you send an idea, we receive your robot's name and the idea you typed or said. Ideas go only to the Bolt Squad team, never to other players. If you use the microphone, your device turns your voice into text. We receive only the text, never a recording.

Playing with friends

When you play with friends, the game connects your device to theirs using a squad code. Players see each other's robot name, look and approved face picture. Chat in the game uses ready-made phrases only, so players cannot type messages to each other. To connect, the game uses PeerJS, a free service that only passes connection details between devices.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) stores cloud saves, the leaderboard, face pictures waiting for approval, and ideas.
  • FormSubmit sends our team an email when there is a new idea or picture to review.
  • PeerJS helps devices find each other for online play.

None of these services use your information for advertising.

Children

Bolt Squad is made for kids and families. We do not ask for anyone's real name, email, phone number or location, and we do not allow open chat. Parents can ask us to see or delete anything their child has shared by emailing robert@vilasi.co.

Deleting your information

To delete everything stored on your device, delete the app or clear the site's data in your browser. To delete your cloud save, leaderboard entries, pictures or ideas, email robert@vilasi.co with your save code or robot name and we will delete them within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
