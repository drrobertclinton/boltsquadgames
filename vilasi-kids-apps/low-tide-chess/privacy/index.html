Privacy Policy

Low Tide Chess, published by Vilasi, Inc. Effective November 7, 2026.

The short version

Low Tide Chess does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no ads, no chat, no purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on the device

  • Stars, badges, which lessons and puzzles are done, and a game in progress.
  • Settings: sounds on or off, and read-aloud on or off.

These are saved only in the app's own storage on the device. They are never sent to Vilasi, Inc. or anyone else. Deleting the app, or choosing Start fresh in the Grown-ups area, removes them.

Internet use

The lessons, puzzles, pictures and fonts are all inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not contact any server and has no outside links.

Read aloud

Miss Odessa's words can be read aloud with the device's built-in voice. This happens on the device; no audio is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

Low Tide Chess is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Children

The app is made for children ages 5 to 14 and follows the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families rules. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to review or delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be inside the app update and on the app's store page before it takes effect.
