Low Tide Chess Support

Published by Vilasi, Inc.

Questions, or a puzzle not working? Email robert@vilasi.co and tell us which device you use and what happened.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no purchases, no ads and no accounts, and nothing is collected.

How do I take back a move?

When playing Miss Odessa, tap Take back under the board. Hint shows a good move in two steps.

How do I start over?

Grown-ups, then Start fresh. It clears stars, badges and the game in progress on that device. Progress stays on the device, so it does not move to a new one.
