What is free?

Book 1 is free, along with Read to me, Words to Know, Dad's Voice recording and the Tide Log. Other books are $2.99 each, $24.99 for all 15, or included with Reader Club ($3.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial). Prices may vary by country.

How do I cancel Reader Club?

iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, your profile icon, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel.

I got a new device. How do I get my books back?

Open Grown-ups, answer the math question, tap Unlock books, then Restore Purchases, using the same store account. Reading progress and recordings stay on the old device.

Refunds

Apple and Google process purchases and handle refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play's refund page.
