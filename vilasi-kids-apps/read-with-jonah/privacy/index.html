Read with Jonah Privacy Policy

Published by Vilasi, Inc. Effective November 7, 2026.

The short version

Read with Jonah does not collect personal information from children. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Book 1 is free; a grown-up can unlock more books behind the parental gate, and the app goes online only to handle those purchases.

What stays on this device

Reading progress, saved words, quiz results, badges, reading time and settings are kept in the app’s own storage on this device so the reader can pick up where they left off.

If a parent uses Dad’s Voice, the narration recordings are saved privately inside the app on this device. They are not uploaded, shared or sent anywhere.

Deleting the app removes all of this information. Reading data can also be cleared at any time from the Grown-ups area.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when a grown-up chooses to record a chapter in Dad’s Voice, which sits behind a parental gate. The app does not listen at any other time.

Read to me

Read to me uses the speech voice built into the device and follows the device’s own speech settings. The app itself sends nothing to Vilasi, Inc. or anyone else.

Children

The app is designed for children ages 8 to 12 and follows the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): it does not collect personal information from children. Parent settings, book purchases and voice recording are behind a parental gate. Links out of the app appear only there.

Purchases

Book 1 is free. A grown-up can unlock more books or join Reader Club in the Grown-ups area, which sits behind the parental gate. Children never see prices or purchase buttons.

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details.

To know which books are unlocked on your family’s devices, the app connects to RevenueCat, Inc., our purchase processor, only for purchases. RevenueCat receives the purchase record from the store (which book or plan, when it was bought and when a plan renews or ends) together with a random app ID created on the device. It does not receive the reader’s name, reading progress, saved words, recordings or any other information from the app, and no attributes or device identifiers are sent. Purchase information is used only to unlock what was bought, not for advertising or tracking, and is not sold.

A parent can manage or cancel Reader Club in the Apple Account or Google Play settings, and use Restore Purchases in the Grown-ups area on a new device. To ask for purchase records to be deleted, write to Vilasi, Inc. at robert@vilasi.co.
