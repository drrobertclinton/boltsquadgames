Low Tide Art: Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Published by Vilasi, Inc., 1475 Midland Road, Unit 41, Southern Pines, NC 28387, United States.

Low Tide Art is a drawing, painting and coloring app made for children, part of the Low Tide family of apps from the Jonah Pruitt books. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Low Tide Art does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or any other data, from children or from anyone else.

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or ages are asked for.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party software that collects data.
  • No chat, messaging, sharing, public gallery or social features.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. On Android it does not even ask for internet permission.
  • There are no links out of the app.

Your child's drawings

Drawings are saved only inside the app on the device, in "My Art". They never leave the device unless a grown-up chooses to save one to the device's photo library. That option sits behind a grown-up question (a parental gate). On iPhone and iPad the app asks only for permission to add photos, never to see the photo library. On Android the grown-up picks the folder, and the app needs no photo or storage permission.

Deleting a drawing in My Art removes it. Deleting the app removes all of its drawings.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete with us, and no parental consent is needed for data collection. The app is designed to meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's Kids Category rules and Google Play's Families Policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect. We will never begin collecting children's data without verifiable parental consent.
