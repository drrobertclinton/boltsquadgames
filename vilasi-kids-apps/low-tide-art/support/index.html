Low Tide Art: Support

Low Tide Art is a drawing, painting and coloring app for children, published by Vilasi, Inc. It works offline and collects no data.

Common questions

  • Where are my drawings? Tap My Art on the home screen. Drawings are saved only on this device.
  • How do I save a drawing to Photos? Open it in My Art and tap Save to Photos. A grown-up answers a math question first. On Android, pick the Pictures folder the first time.
  • I deleted the app. Can I get my drawings back? No. Drawings live only on the device, so deleting the app deletes them. Save favorites to Photos first.
  • How do I use the lessons? Tap Learn to draw (or Bolt Squad for the robots), pick a picture, and draw over the dashed pink lines. Tap Next for the next step, or the eye button to hide the guide.
  • What is Bolt Squad? Bolt Squad is a deep-sea robot game invented by Knox. Low Tide Art teaches kids to draw its robots. The game itself is a separate app, rated 9+.
