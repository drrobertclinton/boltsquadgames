Low Tide Games Support

Published by Vilasi, Inc.

Questions, or a puzzle not working? Email robert@vilasi.co and tell us which device you use and what happened.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no purchases, no ads and no accounts, and nothing is collected.

How do I change the age band?

Tap Grown-ups, answer the math question, and choose who's playing.

How do I start over?

Grown-ups, then Start fresh. It clears stars and badges on that device. Progress stays on the device, so it does not move to a new one.
