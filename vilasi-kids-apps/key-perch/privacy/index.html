Key Perch Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Published by Vilasi, Inc.

Key Perch is an app and web game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Key Perch does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or any other data, from children or anyone else.

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or ages are asked for. Children pick an owl color, not a name.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party software that collects data.
  • No chat, messaging, sharing or social features.
  • No purchases.
  • The app does not send anything over the internet. The only links out of it are this policy and our support page, behind a grown-up question.

What stays on the device

Lessons, stars, speeds and owl choices are saved only on the device or in the browser. The app uses no camera, microphone, photos or location. Key presses are used only to run the lesson and are not stored or sent; only totals such as speed and accuracy are saved on the device. If the Voice setting is on, instructions are read aloud by the voices built into the device. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data for the web version, removes everything.

The web version

The web version is the same game loaded from a web server, and it then runs in the browser. Like any website, the server that delivers its files may keep routine technical logs, such as an IP address and the time of a request, to keep the service running. We do not use those logs to identify or track anyone. The game itself sends nothing back.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete with us, and no parental consent is needed for data collection. The app is designed to meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's Kids Category rules and Google Play's Families Policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect. We will never begin collecting children's data without verifiable parental consent.
