Common questions

Does it need a real keyboard?

It is built for one, because finger habits only form on real keys. Any Bluetooth or wired keyboard works on an iPad or Android tablet, and Chromebooks and computers already have one. Without a keyboard, children can tap the keys on screen to try it out.

Which keyboards does it support?

The standard US layout (QWERTY).

Can the app see if my child uses the right fingers?

No. It sees which keys are pressed, how quickly, and which Shift key was used. It teaches the right fingers with pictures and habits, and a quick look from you helps most.

What if my child already knows some typing?

Tap For grown-ups, answer the math question and choose Open all lessons.

Can brothers and sisters share a device?

Yes. Each child picks an owl, and up to six typers can use one device.

Does Key Perch have ads, purchases or accounts?

No. Key Perch is free, has no ads, no purchases and no accounts, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Where is progress kept?

Only on the device or in the browser. Progress does not move to a new device, and deleting the app removes it.
