Common questions

What age is it for?

Children about 6 to 8 can use it on their own, and the whole family can use it together. The Family Devotional is written to be read aloud by a grown-up.

How does Let's pray work?

Four easy steps in kid words, each with a picture and a Bible verse: Wow, God!, I'm sorry, Thank you and Please help. Tap Read it to me to hear each step.

How do voice prayers work?

On the Family List, open a person and tap Record. The prayer is saved on this device only. A grown-up can send it to someone, such as Grandma, from the Grown-ups area with the phone's Share button.

How do I get into the Grown-ups area?

Open the Grown-ups tab and answer the math question. Children are not able to share recordings or change settings without it.

Which devotional do we read this week?

A new five-minute devotional opens each week, 52 in all, with a verse, a short story, two questions to talk about and a prayer. You can also read any of them from the full list.

The prayer-time reminder is not showing.

In Grown-ups, make sure the reminder is on, then check that notifications are allowed for Where We Pray in the device's settings. On Android, battery-saving modes can delay notifications.

Does Where We Pray have ads, purchases or accounts?

No. It is free, with no ads, no purchases and no accounts, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Where is our list kept? Can we move it to a new phone?

Only on the device. There is no account or cloud copy, so the list, the answered-prayers jar and voice prayers do not move to a new device, and deleting the app removes them.

How do I erase everything?

Grown-ups, answer the question, then Clear everything on this phone. This cannot be undone.
