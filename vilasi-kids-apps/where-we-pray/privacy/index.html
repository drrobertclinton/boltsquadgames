Where We Pray Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. Published by Vilasi, Inc.

Where We Pray is a free app that helps children learn to pray and helps families pray together. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Where We Pray does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or any other data, from children or anyone else.

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address or age.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party software that collects data.
  • No chat, messaging or social features.
  • No purchases.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and has no links out of it.

What stays on the device

Everything a family enters is saved only on the device: the names and topics on the family prayer list, answered prayers in the answered-prayers jar, voice prayers, devotional and prayer progress, and settings such as bigger text. We cannot see any of it.

The microphone and voice prayers

The microphone is used only while a child taps Record on a family list entry. The recording is saved in the app's own folder on the device and is never uploaded. A parent or guardian can choose to send a recording with the device's Share feature; that is only available in the Grown-ups area, behind a parental question. What happens after a grown-up shares is up to them and the app they choose. "Read it to me" uses the voice built into the device.

Reminders

If a grown-up turns on the prayer-time reminder, the device schedules a local notification at the chosen time. No push service or server is involved. It can be turned off in the app or in the device's notification settings.

Deleting information

In the app, open Grown-ups, answer the question and tap Clear everything on this phone, or delete the app. Either removes everything the app stored. Because we never receive any information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete with us, and no parental consent is needed for data collection. The app is designed to meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's Kids Category rules and Google Play's Families Policy.

Scripture

Bible verses are from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is in the public domain.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect. We will never begin collecting children's data without verifiable parental consent.
