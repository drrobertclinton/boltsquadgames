Bolt Squad Support

Need help? We read every message.

Email robert@vilasi.co and tell us your robot's name, what device you use, and what happened. We usually answer within two days.

Common questions

How do I play with friends? Tap Play with Friends, then Start a Squad. Tell your friends the code. They tap Play with Friends, then Join, and type the code.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? Open Your Robot and find Save Your Progress. Copy your save code. On the new phone, open the same spot, tap Restore and type the code.

Why can't other players see my robot's face picture? Every picture is checked by a grown-up on our team before other players can see it. Until then, only you can see it.

I saw a picture that isn't okay. During a meeting, tap the flag next to that player's name. The picture is hidden right away until we check it.

How do I delete my information? Email us your save code or robot name and we will delete your cloud save, leaderboard entries, pictures and ideas. See our privacy policy.

Do I have to pay for anything? No. Bolt Squad is free with no ads. In the apps (version 1.1 and later), a grown-up can buy an optional photo pass so other players can see a robot face picture. Each picture is still checked by our team first, and a denied picture gives the pass back. Refunds go through Apple or Google.

Got an idea for the game?

Use the Idea Box in the game. Ideas go straight to the Bolt Squad team.
