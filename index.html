    How to Play

    Eight robots live in Trench Base, deep under the ocean. One of them is secretly the Glitch.

    AS CREW

    • Walk to your glowing consoles and press USE to play each repair game.
    • Fill the Repairs bar to win.
    • SCAN a robot up close. If it zapped someone in the last 45 seconds, it reads HOT.
    • Find a frozen bot? Press REPORT. Then talk it out and vote the Glitch off the base.
    • Got frozen? You become a ghost. Keep fixing things to help your team.

    AS THE GLITCH

    • Sneak up on a bot when nobody's watching and press ZAP.
    • Press SABOTAGE to crack the hull. If nobody seals it at the Sub Dock in time, you win.
    • Win when only two crew bots are left awake. Don't get voted out.

    PLAYING WITH FRIENDS

    • Tap Play with Friends, then Start a Squad. You get a 5-letter code.
    • Friends open boltsquadgames.com, tap Play with Friends, type your code and tap Join.
    • When everyone's in, tap Start Game. Robots fill any empty spots.
    • In meetings, tap the quick chat buttons to talk. Pick a robot first for messages like "I think it's ___".

    CONTROLS

    Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, E use, R report, Q scan or zap, F sabotage.
    Phone: drag anywhere on the screen to move, tap the round buttons.

    First time playing?

    Take the 2-minute tutorial. You'll learn how to move, fix things, scan robots, report and vote.
